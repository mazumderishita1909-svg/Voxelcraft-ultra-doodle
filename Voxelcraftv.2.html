VOXELCRAFT ULTRA

Optimized 60FPS Infinite Terrain • Day/Night Cycle • Smooth Mobile & Desktop Controls

Controls Guide:
🖥️ Desktop: Click to lock mouse. WASD, Space to jump, F to fight, G near a cow to collect milk or near a chicken to tame it with a seed; tamed chickens lay eggs over time. H near a bed at night to sleep. Left/right click to break/place, E for inventory, C by a chest, Z to undo, Escape to pause.
📱 Mobile: Use Left Virtual Joystick to walk, Right side swipe to look around. On-screen Jump, Break, Place, Fight, Animal & Inventory buttons. Use Animal near cows/chickens; tame chickens with seeds to get eggs over time. Eat milk, eggs or other food from the inventory to restore hunger.
🌀 Portal: Find the glowing portal on the highest nearby mountain and walk into it to reach the lava realm. Use the return portal there to come back.
