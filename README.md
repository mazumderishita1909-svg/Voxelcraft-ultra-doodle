**Voxel Craft** is a lightweight, voxel-based sandbox game where your imagination is the only limit! Explore procedurally generated worlds, break and place blocks, and build your dream creations block by block.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🌍 **Procedural World Generation:** Explore unique landscapes every time you play.
* 🧱 **Block Building & Mining:** Break, collect, and place various types of voxel blocks.
* ⚡ **Fast & Lightweight:** Optimized for smooth performance and quick loading.
* 🎨 **Voxel Aesthetics:** Clean, vibrant, retro-style graphics.
